Secure https:// sites load over TLS 1.3 (sites that only speak older TLS cannot be reached, and certificates are not checked). Type an address in the bar above (Ctrl+L), use Alt+Left to go back, and F5 to reload. wget, ping and ifconfig in a terminal help when a page will not load.
