Columns and drag and drop

Three balanced columns

Multi-column layout cuts a box's contents into columns of even height, side by side. This paragraph and the ones after it are laid out once, as a single column a third of the width, then cut between lines.

A column ends between two lines, never through one; the margin where it ends is dropped, so the next column starts right at the top of its first line.

The rules between the columns are column-rule: one pixel, solid, in the color of the card's border. They are as tall as the tallest column.

column-gap: normal is 1em between columns. Here it is set to 24 pixels.

Resize the window and the columns get narrower, but there are always three of them.
