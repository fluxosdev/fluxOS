Scroll snapping

Turn the wheel over the strip, or use its scroll bar: when the scroll stops, it glides to the nearest slide in the direction you went (scroll-snap-type: x mandatory). Slide 4 has scroll-snap-stop: always, so a big scroll cannot skip past it. Buttons use scrollBy(), which lands on a slide too.

View transitions

document.startViewTransition() shows the old page while the update runs, then moves each element with a view-transition-name from its old place to its new one and cross-fades the rest.

content-visibility: auto

Each of these rows has content-visibility: auto: its contents are laid out only when it comes near the window, and it keeps the size it had once it has been seen. Rows laid out: ? (the count rises as you scroll down).
